## 4. How we protect it

- **Not even afterbox can open your vault.** Your sealed record is protected with split-key encryption. The key is split so that no single party, including us, can reconstruct it alone. Releasing it requires a verified death and your executor's own credential.
- Your bank information is kept separately from your sealed record, and only what's needed is kept.
- We use encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls and regular security reviews.

## 5. Who we share it with

**We never sell your data.** We share information only:

- **With the people you chose, when the time comes,** each seeing only what you left for them.
- **With service providers who help us run afterbox,** such as hosting, storage, payments, email, identity and document checks, and open banking. They act on our instructions and must protect your information.
- **With a professional you ask us to introduce you to,** and only what's needed for that introduction.
- **When the law requires it,** or to protect people from fraud or harm.
- **If afterbox is ever sold or restructured,** the new owner must honour this policy and your record stays sealed.

## 9. After you die

UK data protection law covers living people. Even so, we'll treat your record with the same care after you die, release it only as you chose, and never use it for anything else.
