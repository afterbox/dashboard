# afterbox — strategy summary

Full version: Notion → Justice's Brain → "afterbox: strategy, moat and the path to $1B".

## The problem
- ~600,000 deaths a year in the UK, ~3 million in the US. Each leaves a family with ~400 hours of admin over six months.
- Families miss things: an estimated £15B in UK unclaimed assets (incl. £9.7B pensions); over $1B of US life insurance unclaimed every year.
- With no clear record, families often pay a probate solicitor 2–5% of the estate (£6,000–£15,000 on a £300,000 estate), largely for detective work.
- People know they should prepare but put it off: it feels like confronting death, and there's no deadline.

## What's new
1. A living record people add to over years — practical and personal (messages to named people) in one place.
2. Asset discovery from payment trails via open banking (direct debits reveal policies, pensions, exchanges).
3. Sealed with split-key encryption: not even afterbox can open it; release needs a verified death plus the executor.
4. Guided, calm settlement for the executor; each loved one sees only what was left for them.
5. Framed as a gift for the people you love, not admin.

## Path to $1B
Vault (brand and stories) → family support and employers (early revenue) → insurers and banks (millions of users) → the settlement network institutions plug into (one verified death notice reaches every connected institution; they pay per case).
